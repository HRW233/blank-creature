Nobody holds him up any more: the knees are free hinges and there is no brace holding a leg straight, so standing on two feet is the first thing the brain had to learn. He is never told to walk — he is paid. Every step charges him muscle effort and pays him for standing tall, for keeping his torso upright and for metres gained while upright, and the finish pays a bonus on the time left; crawling earns nothing. Every episode is a hundred-metre race with ten seconds on the clock, against Usain Bolt's 9.58 s record. The weights were evolved offline for 700 generations of twenty-four mutated copies under exactly this reward and are baked in, so everyone who opens this link watches the same runner.
